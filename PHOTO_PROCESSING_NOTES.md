# Burton & Found — photographs in the online catalogue

40 individual item photographs were reprocessed from the original captures, one by one, with adaptive lighting correction, moderate cast-shadow recovery and limited reduction of reflected highlights. The page content, signatures, dates, tears, patina and margins were not regenerated, cloned, redrawn or retouched. No invented details were inserted. The original captures have been saved separately in the *Unedited Product Photos Backup* ZIP for seller records and are not exposed through a product-page toggle.

Limitations: a completely white reflection or heavily darkened area cannot be reliably restored if the camera failed to capture the printed detail. Some glare remains and a fresh photograph under diffuse lighting should be obtained before a final sale if these areas affect condition or authenticity. Listings must not claim that digital retouching restored the original physical item.

Recommended photographing method: remove a removable plastic sleeve when it is safe to do so; place the item flat on matte neutral backing; use two diffused lights at 45 degrees or indirect daylight; hold the camera parallel to the paper; capture the complete sheet, reverse, date, and condition details.

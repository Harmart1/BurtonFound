# Burton & Found — Vintage Automobile Price Research and Curated Sets

**Prepared:** 15 November 2026  
**Market:** Canadian-dollar proposed asking prices for photographed, apparently vintage magazine-page automotive advertising  
**Status:** Internal pricing guidance and public browse-only preview. No 2026 physical inventory inspection, printing/issue provenance, exact dimensions or condition grading has been completed.

## Pricing evidence

These are comparable **asking prices** unless identified otherwise. An asking price is not evidence of a successful sale. Digital reproductions, framed prints and full magazines are different merchandise and were not treated as equivalent to an unframed physical magazine advertising page.

- [eBay Canada, 1960s original car advertisements](https://www.ebay.ca/sch/i.html?_fsrp=1&_nkw=1960s+car+ads&_oaa=1): observed examples range from roughly C$6.99 for an Oldsmobile Vista-Cruiser page to C$24.13 for a Buick page and C$40.22 for a Pontiac page, excluding some delivery charges.
- [Etsy Canada, original vintage magazine advertising](https://www.etsy.com/market/vintage_original_advertisement_ads?page=3): 1955 Dodge original page around C$19.25; 1946 Oldsmobile original page around C$8.89.
- [Etsy Canada, retro automobile advertisements](https://www.etsy.com/market/retro_auto_ad?page=5): 1937 LaSalle page around C$18.80; 1961 Mercury Meteor ad around C$20.63.
- [Etsy Canada, Cadillac advertising](https://www.etsy.com/market/cadillac_vintage_ads): many original-page examples appear around C$14–C$29, depending on design and seller.
- [Flashbacks USA, original 1940s and 1950s US automobile advertisements](https://www.flashbacksusa.com/category/usa-car-ads): published retailer asking prices include standard pages around $10–$25 and selected premium models around $35–$45 (website displays '$'; do not assume Canadian currency).
- [MaxSold: four 1939–1941 Nash LIFE ads](https://maxsold.com/listing/7956403/4-original-nash-car-ads-1939-1941?hideclosed=0): a historical auction lot closed at $3 total on 14 May 2026, illustrating how liquidation outcomes can fall well below dealer asking prices.

Research is not transaction-level sold-comparable analysis. Market prices, listing quality, seller reputation and shipping costs vary. Price proposals should be reviewed against actual paper condition and the reverse side before they become saleable offers.

## Pricing policy

1. **93 individual page-scan previews:** preliminary Canadian-dollar price guidance, generally around C$14.95–C$29.95 each. Brighter illustrated advertising, stronger visual design, some prewar pages and desirable luxury/performance marques receive moderate uplifts. Text-heavy pages are priced more conservatively. These are *not appraisals* or confirmed retail offers.
2. **Suspected duplicate scans:** 13 duplicate-review groups representing 28 page scans are treated as potentially referring to the same physical advertising page. Members of a suspected duplicate group share a suggested price. Repeated scans do not establish additional stock units.
3. **Condition and source:** a PDF scan cannot independently confirm authenticity, paper dimensions, printing date, back-of-page contents, physical wear or ownership. All 93 records use `salesEnabled: false`, `physicalItemVerified: false`, and `suggestedPriceCAD` (not `price`).
4. **Curated sets:** 10 editorially coherent groupings cover 29 distinct scan records with no reused page across the sets. Their suggested group price generally represents about a 10–20% discount to the sum of independent preliminary prices. The sets themselves cannot be purchased or reserved.
5. **Existing inventory:** the 22 Coca-Cola/Old Hollywood listings already had descriptions and prices; their inventory and selling prices were not changed. No prices, quantities or checkout flags were written to Supabase. The shop's live payment capabilities remain disabled.

### Publication note

Descriptions were written from the supplied photographed source pages and the source filenames, not from a confirmed physical paper inspection. They describe imagery and advertising copy visible in the scans. Claims of exact print issue, physical condition, original ownership or rarity have intentionally been avoided. The website shows the complete descriptive text under each image's **More about this page** control.

**Before launch:** inspect actual pages and backs; confirm the exact period, physical format and size; reconcile duplicates; verify each proposed set has distinct units; adjust the condition-specific price; update inventory and legal/tax/shipping disclosures; obtain explicit merchant approval before any sales-enabled changes.

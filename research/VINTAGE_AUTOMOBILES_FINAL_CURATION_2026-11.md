# Burton & Found — Final Automobile Design Audit, Image Curation & Price Review

**Prepared 16 November 2026 · CAD · Suggested asking prices only**

## Executive findings

- **93 source photographs audited** through perceptual image matching, ORB feature/RANSAC consistency and visual examination of actual scan contact sheets.
- **13 redundant page-scan listings consolidated**, leaving **80 distinct pictured advertising designs** in the public archive. All 93 original scan files and the 93 first-pass enhanced files are retained, including the files for duplicate views.
- Two wrongly suspected pairs were restored as separate designs: `VA-003` and `VA-066` have different Lincoln illustrations (only `VA-075` repeats `VA-066`); `VA-027` and `VA-037` are two different Nash advertisements.
- 80 archival-faithful second-pass display derivatives prepared from the best representative scans. Colour/illumination and letterform clarity were gently improved; `VA-072` received a separately preserved straightened view. Each listing provides access to its unmodified original scan.
- **Individual suggested asking price range C$12.95–C$24.95**, median C$17.95.
- **18 curated proposed sets** cover 53 unique pictured designs without overlap; the remaining 27 are better displayed as stand-alone choices for now.
- Every original and curated listing remains **under review, non-purchasable**. No physical item counts, authenticity, reverse-side content, page measurements or grades were inferred from photographs.

## Research and valuation basis

Price recommendations have been recalibrated below many earlier proposals to fit ordinary Canadian vintage print-advertising asking prices while reserving modest premiums for especially appealing illustrated designs, distinctive early print, Cadillac/Lincoln/Thunderbird models and visually strong layouts.

Relevant comparable **asking-price** evidence (not proven sale transactions):

- [Treasure Valley Antiques, Canada](https://treasurevalleyantiques.com/collections/automobilia?page=11) lists various original Oldsmobile page ads around C$9.99–C$14.99.
- [Angela's Vintage Ads and More, eBay Canada](https://www.ebay.ca/str/sellingitall65/Magazine-Ads-CHRYSLER/_i.html?_pgn=1&store_cat=35708666018) lists numerous Chrysler originals around C$14 each.
- [Jay's Mercantile, Saskatoon](https://jaysmercantile.ca/collections/automotive-farm-industrial?page=2) lists a 1960s Opel advertising page for C$30 and a three-page Body by Fisher set for C$40.
- [Vintage Ads and Books](https://vintageadsandbooks.com/vintage-automobile-ads) lists some 1934 Buick originals around US$9 during promotions (USD and promotional pricing, not directly comparable to CAD).
- [Flashbacks USA](https://www.flashbacksusa.com/category/usa-car-ads) displays original US car advertising at varied prices, including Thunderbird and Buick pages around US$10–$20 and certain rare/desirable cars higher.

Comparability is limited by age, page dimensions, print condition, back-of-page material, whether artwork is an actual original, whether framed, and shipping costs. Online asking prices are not authenticated sold comparables or formal appraisals. Seller may approve final prices after physically viewing and grading every advertisement.

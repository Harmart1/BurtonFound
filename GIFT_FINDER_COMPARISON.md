# Gift finder and item comparison

The shop now includes a collapsed interactive gift finder (collection, CAD budget, item/set format) and three-item comparison. Live public stock and prices take precedence; unverified automobile designs and unknown stock require explicit inclusion, sold/reserved and CMS-only drafts are excluded from gift suggestions. Shared physical catalogue IDs are de-duplicated in suggestions and flagged when compared. Comparisons remain page-local; no personal data, account, storage, marketing or checkout requests. The existing evergreen gift guide links directly to the interactive finder. Existing product details and checkout remain authoritative. Unit tests and isolated desktop/mobile browser scenarios cover the tools. No purchase, shipping or delivery promise is made.

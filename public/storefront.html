A NOTE FROM THE CABINET

Good things
don't have to be new

Based in Sault Ste. Marie, Ontario, Burton & Found has a soft spot for printed things that were meant to be temporary. The magazine advertisement, the postcard, the ticket stub: small records of what the world once looked like.

We're building a carefully described collection of vintage paper ephemera—interesting enough to keep, honest about its age and about what hasn't yet been authenticated. Each piece has its own little history. We hope you'll find one that feels like yours.

Burton — The collection

FIELD NOTES NO. 001

For the love of old paper

A collector's guide to what you're actually buying, and how we protect it on its way to you.

✳

Original means original

Our Coca-Cola advertisements are period magazine pages. Hollywood portraits and film images can have different printing histories; uncertain dates and sources are clearly flagged.

⌁

Age tells its own story

Vintage paper can show gentle toning, tiny marks, or edge wear. We keep the honest details in each listing and don't digitally present an item as restored.

✉

Careful to the last mile

We plan to ship flat paper with rigid protection. Postage, tax and applicable service options must be confirmed before any order is paid. We are based in Sault Ste. Marie, Ontario.

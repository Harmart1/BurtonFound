Manual shipping checklist

  1. Confirm payment status is paid and inspect the item before packing
  2. Pack paper in a protective sleeve and rigid, weather-resistant mailer; use a box for books or fragile collectibles
  3. Get a real tracked postage quote and purchase a label using Canada Post Snap Ship
  4. Enter carrier and tracking in the order, mark it Shipped, and save
  5. Use Prepare update email to notify the buyer manually

This dashboard does not send email, buy postage, retrieve live delivery estimates, or process refunds. Downloaded CSV files include personal addresses; store and delete them securely.

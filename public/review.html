What helps us most?

We want the shop to feel welcoming to longtime collectors and people browsing vintage finds for the first time.

  1. Is it easy to understand what is actually for sale?
  2. Are prices, condition and authenticity notes clear?
  3. Does the site feel trustworthy on your phone or computer?
  4. Would you return for books or collectibles as they arrive?

No email address or name is required to give feedback. If you choose email, your email app controls delivery.

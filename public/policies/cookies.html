Cookies & Browser Storage Notice

Effective 20 November 2026 · Last updated 28 November 2026

A practical explanation of on-device preferences, account sessions and technologies used by the storefront.

What is used on this site

Cookies are small data values a website or provider can set in your browser. “Local storage” is a browser feature that can remember values such as display preferences or selected products. Some technologies are needed to provide the site; others would require additional notice and consent if introduced.

Local preferences and bags

The current site uses browser local storage to remember your shopping bag, guest watchlist, saved guest bags, and display or interface preferences, including light/dark mode where chosen. These are intended to support features you request; they are not a substitute for an account record or proof of an order. Clearing site data or using private browsing may remove this information.

Account sessions and technical logs

When the customer-account service is configured and you sign in, the authentication provider (Supabase) may persist authentication tokens and session information on your device. Account data may also be processed through its cloud service. Cloudflare, the site's hosting and security provider, may collect technical data and use cookies or similar mechanisms to operate and protect the service. The site's web pages may load functional resources from a content-delivery network when account functionality is enabled.

Advertising, analytics and future changes

The current repository does not intentionally install a Meta Pixel, Google Analytics tag or a third-party behavioural-advertising tracker. Organic social-share buttons act only when clicked; opening an external platform may cause that platform to apply its own cookies under its policies. If we activate advertising pixels, analytics or other non-essential tracking that requires consent, we will update this notice and put suitable consent controls in place first. We do not claim that third-party websites are cookie-free.

Your controls

You can clear or block site storage through your browser's privacy or site-data settings. You can choose whether to sign in, avoid optional services, or change your theme and saved-item selections. Restricting essential storage may prevent saved bags, theme settings or authentication from working. These choices do not affect statutory privacy rights. See our Privacy Policy for access, correction and questions about information processed by the business.

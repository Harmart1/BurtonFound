Shipping, Returns & Consumer Rights

Effective 20 November 2026 · Last reviewed 20 November 2026

How availability, packaging, delivery costs, condition issues, cancellations and refunds will be handled when purchasing becomes available.

Current status — no online purchases

Burton & Found is presently a browsing and enquiry storefront. Online card checkout is disabled. A bag or product enquiry is not an order and no online payment should be submitted through this site at present. We will not promise an unconfigured shipping quote or a guaranteed delivery date. The final operational shipping and discretionary return details must be confirmed before accepting online orders.

Availability, pricing and agreement formation

Items may be one of a kind. Listed prices are in Canadian dollars (CAD) unless expressly stated otherwise and remain subject to confirmation before an order is accepted. We will identify applicable taxes, delivery charges, any mandatory fees and the total payable amount before asking a consumer to accept a purchase. If an item is unavailable or materially misdescribed, we will explain the issue and not substitute a different item without agreement.

For qualifying online consumer agreements in Ontario, the Consumer Protection Act, 2002 and Ontario Regulation 17/05 impose advance disclosure, a clear opportunity to accept or decline and correct errors, and delivery of a copy of the agreement. Those obligations cannot be avoided by site terms.

Shipping destinations, rates and dispatch

The current preview is configured for Canada-only fulfilment, with live carrier quotes disabled. The carrier, service, dispatch estimate, postage, package dimensions, insurance or tracking availability and destination eligibility must be confirmed before payment when orders become available. We will not represent carrier estimates as guaranteed delivery dates. International delivery, if subsequently offered, may involve customs charges or destination taxes, which must be disclosed appropriately.

Fragile magazine pages will be assessed for suitable protective packaging such as rigid backing, weather protection and support against bending. Actual packaging and handling are confirmed for each order rather than guaranteed for every item in advance.

Condition and photographing vintage items

Expect honest disclosure of visible age-related wear such as marks, fading, tears, foxing, trimming, folds or reverse-side printing. A catalogue image is not a substitute for item-specific condition information, and photographic colours may vary between screens. Ask for measurements or additional photographs before purchase if a detail matters. “Vintage,” “used,” “unframed” or “sold as seen” wording does not remove consumer rights under applicable law.

Changing your mind; discretionary returns

Ontario law does not ordinarily create a universal change-of-mind return right for every sale of goods. Burton & Found has not yet established an optional change-of-mind return window, exchange service or prepaid return-label programme. Before any order is accepted, we will state clearly whether a voluntary return or exchange is available for that particular purchase, what time limits and conditions apply, and who pays any voluntary return shipping.

Any such voluntary policy is additional to, and does not override, statutory cancellation, rescission, warranty or other legal remedies.

Defective, misdescribed, damaged or missing goods

If a delivered item does not match a material description, arrives damaged, or does not arrive as agreed, notify Burton & Found promptly with the product reference, relevant photographs and delivery details. We will investigate, work with any carrier as appropriate and provide remedies required by law. A consumer is not required to surrender mandatory legal rights simply because a collectible is old, fragile or unique.

Ontario cancellation, delivery and refunds

For an internet agreement covered by Ontario's Consumer Protection Act, 2002 (generally where the consumer's total potential payment obligation exceeds $50), consumers may have cancellation rights if prescribed pre-contract disclosure or a compliant copy of the agreement was not provided. Other cancellation rights may arise from unfair practices, delivery failures or other statutory conditions. Where cancellation is legally effective, applicable refund and goods-return obligations and timelines apply.

Customers can review Ontario's contract cancellation guidance and returns and warranty guidance. Nothing here limits those rights.

Send written notice describing your order or enquiry, the issue and the remedy sought. Retain your confirmation and any delivery or photograph records. The published correspondence address is below.

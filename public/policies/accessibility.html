Accessibility & Customer Service

Effective 20 November 2026 · Last updated 27 November 2026

Our commitment to clear, accessible shopping information, usable site controls and accommodation requests.

Accessibility commitment

Burton & Found aims to make its website, product information and customer interactions usable for people with disabilities, consistent with Ontario's Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act, 2005 (AODA), applicable accessibility standards and the Ontario Human Rights Code. We aim to provide service with dignity, independence, integration and equal opportunity.

Website and mobile experience

The site is designed to support keyboard navigation, readable headings, clear focus states, responsive layouts, visible contrast, alternative text for product images where appropriate, and modal policy windows that can be closed with a button or the Escape key. We aim to identify and address barriers over time; we cannot guarantee that every page, historical image or third-party resource is fully accessible in all combinations of devices and assistive technologies.

Alternative formats and help

If a product photograph, description, account action, document or site feature is inaccessible, please tell us what you need. We will work with you to provide accessible information or communication supports where required, in consultation with you about the appropriate format, without charging more for the accessible format than for the standard one. We can also discuss accessible ways of receiving product information and making enquiries.

Feedback, support and service changes

We welcome accessibility feedback, including reports of keyboard traps, unreadable text, missing image descriptions or inaccessible forms. Explain the page or feature affected and your preferred response format. We will consider the feedback and communicate what we can do. If a service on which customers rely becomes unavailable, we will provide appropriate notice and assistance where required by applicable law.

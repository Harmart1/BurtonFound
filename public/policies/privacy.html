Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026 · Last reviewed 20 November 2026

What information Burton & Found collects, why we use it, who may process it, and how to exercise privacy rights.

Who operates this website

Burton & Found is an independent collectibles storefront based in Sault Ste. Marie, Ontario, Canada. In this policy, “we,” “our” and “us” refer to the business operating Burton & Found. This policy applies to the website, its customer-account features when available, enquiries, and related fulfilment activities. A separate service provider's privacy notice may also apply when you use their service.

Information we collect

  • Browsing and technical information: requests for pages and API resources, approximate network details such as IP address, browser/device information, error or security logs, and information the hosting provider needs to deliver and protect the website.
  • Guest choices: bag contents, saved products, theme preferences and certain browsing settings stored in your browser.
  • Collector accounts, if enabled: email address, authentication records, optional name and preferences, watchlist, saved bags, saved addresses and account changes. Password handling is managed by the account authentication provider; we do not ask you to email us passwords.
  • Enquiries: details you choose to send us, such as a product reference, contact details and questions about condition or delivery.
  • Orders, if purchasing becomes available: the information reasonably needed to fulfil an order, including items, delivery information, payment status, taxes, carrier details and relevant correspondence. Payment-card details would be processed by a hosted payment provider rather than stored in this site's public code.
  • Optional marketing preferences: an express subscription choice, if a compliant mailing-list service is later activated. A collector account or watchlist alone is not a newsletter subscription.

Do not provide sensitive personal information unless it is necessary to resolve a specific request.

How we use information

We use appropriate information to display products, remember chosen preferences, provide optional accounts, respond to enquiries, manage purchase requests, prevent fraud and abuse, operate the website securely, fulfil lawful transactions if enabled, meet accounting or other legal obligations and answer requests about your data. We limit collection to what is reasonably necessary for disclosed purposes.

We do not sell customer personal information. We do not treat signing up for a collector account as consent to promotional emails. We will seek meaningful consent for new purposes where the law requires it.

Service providers and possible processing outside Canada

Our technology uses Cloudflare for hosting and delivery. Account authentication and saved account records are designed to use Supabase when account features are enabled. A third-party JavaScript delivery service may supply the Supabase account library when required. If card checkout is activated, Stripe would process payments. Shipping service providers would receive the shipment details necessary to quote or deliver an order when those services are enabled.

These providers may process information in Canada or in other countries. Where information is processed outside Canada, foreign courts, law enforcement and other authorities may be able to access it under their laws. We remain responsible for appropriate oversight of processing carried out for us. We do not disclose personal information to unrelated businesses for their own promotional use without a lawful basis.

Cookies and browser storage

The current storefront uses browser storage to preserve bag contents, guest watchlists and display preferences. If account sign-in is available, the authentication service may save session information on your device. Basic hosting or security technologies may process technical identifiers. The site's current source does not deliberately include third-party advertising pixels or a behavioural-advertising platform. This is not a promise that your browser will never receive a service-provider cookie or that future optional technologies cannot be introduced.

For practical control instructions, read our Cookies & Browser Storage Notice. If optional analytics or targeted advertising is introduced, we will reassess disclosure and consent requirements first.

Marketing communications

We will not send promotional email merely because you browse, enquire, create an account or save a product. If optional marketing is activated, we will use consent and any applicable statutory exceptions in accordance with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation. Commercial messages will identify the sender and contain a working unsubscribe option. You may withdraw marketing consent without losing access to unrelated website services.

Keeping information and safeguarding it

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for its disclosed purpose or applicable legal, accounting, security or dispute-resolution requirements. Different information may be retained for different periods. Guest browser records remain until you or your browser remove them; deleting them does not necessarily delete data already held in a signed-in account. Where possible, information no longer needed is securely deleted or de-identified.

We use proportionate safeguards, access controls and hosting protections; no internet service can guarantee absolute security. If a security incident occurs, we will assess it and take required steps, including recording and reporting or notifying people where Canadian privacy law requires.

Your choices and privacy requests

Subject to legal exceptions, you may request access to information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccuracies, inquire about our use or disclosure, and withdraw consent where permitted. You can also change browser permissions and delete local website data; account holders may have in-account controls for profiles, watches, addresses and marketing preferences. Some records must be retained for lawful reasons even after account closure.

We aim to respond to access requests within the timelines provided by applicable privacy law, ordinarily 30 days under PIPEDA unless a lawful extension applies. We may need to verify identity before disclosing account information. If you are dissatisfied with our response you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children and uploaded research photographs

The shop is intended for a general audience and is not designed to solicit information from children. A parent or guardian should assist any child who cannot provide meaningful consent. The Paper Trail research feature is intended to process user-selected photographs in the visitor's browser; do not upload images containing other people's personal information without authority. Any future server-side image upload or third-party analysis would require an updated notice before deployment.

Changes, contacts and governing privacy law

This policy is effective 20 November 2026 and may be updated to reflect material changes in services or applicable law. Material changes in how personal information is used will be communicated, and additional consent obtained, where required. Ontario-based commercial activities are generally subject to Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and other applicable laws. This is a public-facing description, not a waiver of privacy rights.

For a question, access or correction request, complaint, cancellation or accessibility request, write to Burton & Found at the correspondence address above, identifying the matter and how we may reply. A monitored business email address has not yet been configured on this website. Do not send payment-card numbers or other sensitive information through general correspondence.

Further reading: Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada — Privacy Guide for Businesses.

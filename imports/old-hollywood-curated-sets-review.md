## Release checklist before activating sales

1. Confirm each physical piece exists and determine whether apparently separate scans are fronts/backs or additional views of one item. Resolve potential duplicates and map each approved sale unit to exactly one SKU (and exactly one set if bundled).
2. Photograph both sides, record actual dimensions, condition (including damage), source/publisher/date when substantiated, and distinguish magazine page from original studio photo or movie poster.
3. Validate bundle composition and confirm pieces have not already been sold separately or assigned to another active SKU. Reserve bundled stock atomically if sets are eventually offered with individual purchases.
4. Approve CAD pricing and package dimensions, and confirm shipping/carrier integration before enabling any paid product in Supabase. Check image publication rights as applicable.
5. Keep `LIVE_CHECKOUT_ENABLED`, `LIVE_PAYMENTS_APPROVED`, `TAX_CONFIGURATION_APPROVED`, and `CARRIER_QUOTES_ENABLED` unchanged during this editorial release.

# Storefront design decisions and research

- Both active collections, Coca-Cola advertising and Old Hollywood, receive equal-sized visual cards on the home page and direct first-level navigation. Baymard (updated October 2026): https://baymard.com/research-articles/ecommerce-navigation-best-practice
- The main catalogue follows immediately, with simple collection, date, type, price-sort and search controls. Baymard: https://baymard.com/research/ecommerce-product-lists
- Text uses high-contrast ink against warm ivory, red-brown accents only with light text, readable system sans for body and controls, archival serif for headings. W3C WCAG 2.2: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/
- Product images are actual items and use flat neutral backgrounds with minimal decorative effects. Any corrected display images preserve printed wording and visible condition.
- Free system fonts are used, with no external font hosting, paid photography app, or analytics dependencies.
- Books and miscellaneous collectibles remain visible as coming-soon categories until real inventory is provided; no fake listings added.

# Collector discovery and retention
Related finds use the current listing's collection, subject tags and approximate era; saved-item suggestions are opt-in on the current page and use explicit watchlist choices only. No browsing history, cookies, new storage, pixels, analytics service, marketing contact or email alerts are added. Recommendations exclude known sold/reserved inventory, already-saved or bagged items and overlapping bundle components; unknown stock is labelled, and automobile designs stay enquiry-only. Suggestions do not reserve stock or authorize checkout.

The storefront exposes a compact saved-finds shelf for guests and signed-in collectors. The account watchlist supports text search, current stock filtering and on-demand refresh from the public catalogue. Prices use the current public feed where applicable. Adding items from the account checks known sold/reserved status and overlapping physical units. Full checkout validation remains server-side. No live payment settings are changed. Actual customer alert emails remain disabled until a verified email provider and consent/unsubscribe procedure are approved.

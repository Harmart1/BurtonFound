# Enquiry and promotional drafts

Purchase and general catalogue enquiries use a local draft composer with SKU, listed asking price, stock caveats, topic selections, optional question, copy and text download. The site never sends or stores an enquiry. Email draft action is available only when sellerEmail is configured; no verified contact channel yet, no central case register. No reservation or checkout is triggered by a draft. Weekly marketing kit also generates 40 square/portrait PNG drafts using the actual unretouched source photographs, fitted rather than cropped. Six automobile picks per weekly rotation carry enquiry-only caveats; no price or stock guarantees. Manifest links to product pages. No automatic posting or customer email. Photo rights, availability and final copy require review. CI tests image format and dimensions; visual design is an editorial default rather than a user-approved template. The public performance monitor probes the actual linked .html collection path; redirects remain same-origin and bounded. A separate browser test waits for fragment navigation before asserting it.
